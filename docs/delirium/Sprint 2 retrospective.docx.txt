Role(s): Game Designer
What went well
The work we have produced thus far has been of very high quality, the game design document was completed very quickly and in great detail, and the concept art produced so far is of professional quality. It is clear everyone in the group is an expert in their respective role, so I am hopeful for what we can all do together, as long as we keep a clear vision of what we’re making. 
What didn't go well
Many ideas spoken about in sprint 1 were simply disregarded this sprint without much communication to the rest of the team, and it seems as though everyone has a different idea of what the game were making at this point is even after a detailed game design document was made. The planner could have been utilised more, and the things that weren’t added weren’t in great detail, as mentioned through lecturer feedback. 
Solutions/best practices
Going forwards I will do weekly checkups to ensure that everyone is on the same page about what we’re making, and I will encourage use of the game design document as a reference for anyone with questions about what to do in this project. On top of this I will also personally ensure the planenr is kept up to date with detaled reflections of my contributions, whilst adding tasks and completing tasks for people whenever possible.
