What went well
This sprint the team was much better at communication, and overall we pulled together to ensure we made a video game we all were happy with. We did also have a small issue with github corrupting some game files, but these were resolved almost instantly
Solutions/best practices
I would say a lot of the issues faced this sprint are easily fixable by just keeping on top of what we have been doing the entire term, that is, ensuring files are in their correct places, and are usable in engine. This, to me, would help us create in future, an even better game.
