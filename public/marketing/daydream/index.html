The Hyperbolic Reality

Daydream Believer is an interactive experience that simulates a normal school day through the lens of ADHD.

It is not a clinical simulation, but a hyperbolic expression of enhanced focus, enhanced lack of focus, and enhanced creativity. The world is stark, white, and overwhelming, until something catches your eye.

> PLAYER: Purple Character (The Self)
> SETTING: Ousedale School (Memory/Trace)
> GOAL: Survive until the bell rings.
> OBSTACLE: Your own brain processing power.
VISUAL STYLE: SUPERHOT x CONFUSION
GEOMETRIC
DISTORTION

The world is traced from real 3D data of Ousedale School. Distant objects distort via a custom Toon Shader to represent lack of focus.

Manual Existence

VISUAL

01. Hyperfocus

The world is white and blurry. Objects of interest are RED.

When you approach a Red object, it turns White and clear. This represents the "Hyperfocus" tunnel vision that guides the player through the chaos.

INPUT

02. Manual Walking

Nothing happens automatically. Movement is an effort.

  • Press Q/W for Left Foot.
  • Press E for Right Foot.
  • If you stop inputting, you stop moving.
SOCIAL

03. Social Coding

TEACHERS (Authority / Blue)
FRIENDS (Interest / Pink)
STRANGERS (Noticed / Red)
STRANGERS (Ignored / White)
SURVIVAL

04. Manual Breathing

You must consciously take every breath.

Press [SPACE] to inhale. If you forget for 10 seconds, the screen fades to black and you pass out.

RENDER

05. Distance Distortion

Object permanence is a struggle.

A custom Toon Shader distorts objects the further they are from the player. You can only clearly see what is immediately in front of you.

AUDIO

06. Adaptive Audio

The soundscape shifts with your focus.

15+ composed tracks change instruments and tempo based on stress levels. Voice lines are heavily processed with reverb to simulate internal dissociation.

Artist Statement

Daydream Believer is an interactive experience that immerses players in what would be a normal school day for most, but through the lens of someone with ADHD.

The game features a hyperbolic representation of the disorder based on research I conducted with real people. These insights were translated into playable mechanics, incorporating enhanced focus, enhanced lack of focus, and enhanced creativity.

The movement system is intentionally unique, requiring players to manually input each step. This reinforces the struggle of executive function.

Audio Composition

  • > 15+ Original compositions created using Piano -> MIDI -> BandLab -> Unreal.
  • > Voice lines recorded by the creator, then altered using ElevenLabs to change vocal timbre while keeping intonation.
  • > Heavy use of Reverb and Spatial Audio to simulate dissociation.

Technical Tech

  • Toon Shader: Custom shader that heavily distorts geometry based on camera distance, simulating the inability to focus on the background.
  • Brain Texture: A 50,000 x 50,000 pixel World Aligned Texture created from heightmaps in WorldEngine, converted to contour lines to resemble "brain folds" on all walls.
  • Rigging: Custom Low-Poly human models with 3-point finger articulation.
  • Chaos Engine: Physics simulation used specifically for the Level 9 wall fracture/spaceship crash.
